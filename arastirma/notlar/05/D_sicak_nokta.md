## Facts with sources (to be verified by full reads)
- O'Connor et al. 2013 (AWI EPIC record of Hawaiian-Emperor Seamount Chain ages): age-distance slope 57 +/- 2 km/Myr between ~57 and 25 Ma over the central ~1900 km; Hawaiian age progression faster since ~15 Ma. Source: https://epic.awi.de/id/eprint/35519/ (snippet, UNVERIFIED until full read)
- Hawaiian-Emperor Bend near 47 Ma; hotspot drift alone cannot explain it. Source: https://www.mn.uio.no/geo/english/research/news-and-events/news/from-the-archive/2017/mysterious-hawaiian-volcano-islands.html (snippet)
- Davies 1988 (via Togia thesis): constant swell width 1000 km with Crough 1983 heights gives buoyancy flux 6300 kg/s. Sleep 1990: 8700 kg/s. Source: https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/earthsciences/academics/theses/H.Togia_Thesis.pdf (secondary, snippet)
- Wessel 1993 JGR 98(B9) 16095-16104, doi 10.1029/93JB01230: geoid/topography high in SE chain, falls off away from hotspot. Elastic thickness 33 km (secondary). Source: https://earthref.org/ERR/39754/ (snippet)
- Schoene et al. 2019 Science (doi 10.1126/science.aau2422): four high-volume Deccan pulses, each ~100 kyr, total ~700-800 kyr (secondary via 2021 reanalysis, https://gchron.copernicus.org/articles/3/181/2021/). Volume/area not in snippet.
- Bryan and Ernst 2008: LIP = mainly mafic, >75% volume in 1-5 Myr pulse; thresholds >0.1 Mkm2 area, >0.1 Mkm3 volume, duration <50 Myr (secondary). Ernst 2014 book, Cambridge.
- Bryan et al. 2010 Earth-Sci Rev: eruptions >=1000 km3 DRE possible in LIPs.

## Primary reads done (2026-11-12)
- O'Connor et al. 2013 (AWI EPIC abstract, read): https://epic.awi.de/id/eprint/35519/ . Age-distance slope 57 +/- 2 km/Myr, central ~1900 km, ~57-25 Ma. Derived: 57 km/Myr = 5.7 cm/yr hotspot-relative (our arithmetic, not stated in source). Volcanism in HEB started >=47.5 Ma and lasted >=5 Myr. Hawaiian age progression faster since ~15 Ma. Hot spot motion change ~55-50 Ma.
- USGS Mauna Kea (read): https://www.usgs.gov/volcanoes/mauna-kea : summit elevation 4,207 m, shield, most recent eruption 6,000-4,000 yr ago. "Upper flanks twice as steep as Mauna Loa."
- Total height from seafloor: NOT confirmed by a primary source. Guinness (https://guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/tallest-mountain) gives ~10,205 m total, 4,205 m above sea level (search snippet). Base definition contested. Submarine part ~6 km (one source, snippet). USGS FAQ (https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/how-big-are-hawaiian-volcanoes) not fetched.
- Laske et al. 2011 GJI 187:1725 (UCSD copy): https://igppweb.ucsd.edu/~gabi/papers/gji.dec11-all.pdf . Read intro: swell mechanisms (Davies 1988; Sleep 1990; Wessel 1993 cited as plume dragging). Swell width/height numbers to grep further.
- Hassan et al. 2020 Geoscience Frontiers 11:1669 (East African topography): https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10228133 . MODEL result: domal uplift radius 700 km, amplitude >1200 m at eruption, "comparable to inferred uplift (He 2003; Pik 2003, 2008; Burke & Gunnell 2008)". Post-eruption swell amplitude 800-1000 m (model). Afar plume model with 1000 km initial plume head radius.
- Neal, Coffin, Sager 2019 Oceanography 32(1):176 (read): https://tos.org/oceanography/assets/docs/32-1_neal.pdf . OJP area 1.86 Mkm2 (Coffin & Eldholm 1994). OJP volume 44.4-56.7 Mkm3 (Gladczenko et al. 1997, cited). Kerguelen+Broken Ridge area ~2.3 Mkm2 "stand 2 km to 4 km above surrounding ocean floor", thick mafic crust 15-25 km, volume 15-24 Mkm3 (Coffin & Eldholm 1994). Shatsky Rise ~0.5 Mkm2, volume 6.9 Mkm3 (Zhang 2016). Kerguelen plume record ~130 Myr.
- Ross et al. 2005 JVGR 145:281 (read): https://espace.inrs.ca/id/eprint/1903/ (PDF via encoded URL). Table of flood provinces (A = 10^6 km2, V = 10^6 km3, PRESERVED vs original not separated in table):

  Afro-Arabia 31-25 Ma, A 0.6, V 0.35
  North Atlantic 62-55 Ma, A 1.3, V 6.6
  Deccan 65.5 Ma, A 0.5 (ref 9) or 0.8 (ref 10); V 0.5-1 (ref 11), >1.5 (ref 7)
  Ontong Java ~122 Ma, A 1.86, V 8.4   <-- DISAGREES with Neal 2019 (44-57 Mkm3). Note.
  Parana-Etendeka 134-129 Ma, A 1.5, V >1

  Siberian: text says original volume "1-4 x 10^6 km3" (Campbell 1992; Renne 1995; Sharma 1997), actual flood-lava area on Siberian platform ~3 x 10^5 km2 (preserved). Lavas ~37% of total volume, intrusions 37%, volcaniclastics 26%.
  Karoo+Ferrar combined original volume ~1.5 x 10^6 km3 (Eldholm & Coffin 2000, cited in Ross).
- Wessel, Sandwell, Kim 2010 Oceanography 23(1):24 (read PDF copy): https://tos.org/oceanography/assets/docs/23-1_wessel.pdf (Wessel et al. 2010 PDF copy, same paper). Altimetry catalogues hold ~13,000 seamounts (>~1.5 km detectable). Wessel 2001 ~15,000 revised to ~12,000 after duplicates. Predict >100,000 seamounts >1 km uncharted; speculatively 25 million >100 m. Kitchingman et al. 2007: as many as 142,000 >100 m. CORRECTION: earlier memory figure "14,500 above 1000 m" is NOT supported by the paper. Discard.
