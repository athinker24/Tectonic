## Facts with sources (to be verified by full reads)
- O'Connor et al. 2013 (AWI EPIC record of Hawaiian-Emperor Seamount Chain ages): age-distance slope 57 +/- 2 km/Myr between ~57 and 25 Ma over the central ~1900 km; Hawaiian age progression faster since ~15 Ma. Source: https://epic.awi.de/id/eprint/35519/ (snippet, UNVERIFIED until full read)
- Hawaiian-Emperor Bend near 47 Ma; hotspot drift alone cannot explain it. Source: https://www.mn.uio.no/geo/english/research/news-and-events/news/from-the-archive/2017/mysterious-hawaiian-volcano-islands.html (snippet)
- Davies 1988 (via Togia thesis): constant swell width 1000 km with Crough 1983 heights gives buoyancy flux 6300 kg/s. Sleep 1990: 8700 kg/s. Source: https://www.soest.hawaii.edu/earthsciences/academics/theses/H.Togia_Thesis.pdf (secondary, snippet)
- Wessel 1993 JGR 98(B9) 16095-16104, doi 10.1029/93JB01230: geoid/topography high in SE chain, falls off away from hotspot. Elastic thickness 33 km (secondary). Source: https://earthref.org/ERR/39754/ (snippet)
- Schoene et al. 2019 Science (doi 10.1126/science.aau2422): four high-volume Deccan pulses, each ~100 kyr, total ~700-800 kyr (secondary via 2021 reanalysis, https://gchron.copernicus.org/articles/3/181/2021/). Volume/area not in snippet.
- Bryan and Ernst 2008: LIP = mainly mafic, >75% volume in 1-5 Myr pulse; thresholds >0.1 Mkm2 area, >0.1 Mkm3 volume, duration <50 Myr (secondary). Ernst 2014 book, Cambridge.
- Bryan et al. 2010 Earth-Sci Rev: eruptions >=1000 km3 DRE possible in LIPs.
