# A. Anatomy and numbers of collision belts (working notes, 2026-11-11)
Format: fact, then source URL. Anything not read in a source is marked UNVERIFIED.

## HIMALAYA (central Nepal / Ganges foreland)
Source: DeCelles & Carrapa 2021 JGR Solid Earth 126, e2020JB021256 (full text read) https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Indian basement flexed ~5-6.5 km below sea level directly adjacent to Main Frontal thrust (MFT). https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Foredeep fill (Miocene to modern) thickens from 1000 to ~5000 m over ~190 km south-to-north. https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Flexural rigidity used for foreland plate: 3.0e23 to 1.0e24 N m, loaded by half-loads giving average topography 150-200 km wide and 4 km high. https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Present taper, central Nepal (own 16 DEM profiles, ~16.5 km spacing, ~150 km long, N18.5E): surface slope alpha ~0.1-0.55 deg in foredeep/Chitwan wedge-top basin; Lesser Himalaya average ~1.6 deg; Greater Himalaya average ~7.4-7.6 deg (range 5.3-15.8 deg). Steep Greater Himalayan south face is only 20-40 km wide. https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Basal decollement dip beta: ~3 deg under proximal foreland basin, ~3.5 deg under Chitwan basin, ~6.6 deg under Lesser Himalaya, ~9.1 deg north of Main Central Thrust (>10 deg at rear). Decollement depth ~6.5 km at 30 km north of the front, ~11 km at 60 km; >18 km north of ~80 km. https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- Total taper (alpha+beta): undeformed foredeep ~2.6-3.1 deg; Chitwan + Lesser Himalaya ~4-8 deg; Lesser Himalaya 6.5-8.5 deg. Greater Himalaya alpha>7 deg and beta>9 deg is incompatible with a purely brittle critical wedge (brittle-ductile transition, citing Williams 1994). https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963
- MFT propagated ~85 km southward in early Miocene into foredeep, followed by ~42 km shortening of those rocks; Main Boundary thrust slip ~100 km order. https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10229963

## ZAGROS (Arabia-Eurasia)
Source: Mouthereau 2011 Geol. Mag. 148, 726-738 "Timing of uplift in the Zagros belt/Iranian plateau and accommodation of late Cenozoic Arabia-Eurasia convergence" (full text read) https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/19E9DEA9D3C59A77EFB14BFA4B36A1E2/S0016756811000306a.pdf/timing_of_uplift_in_the_zagros_beltiranian_plateau_and_accommodation_of_late_cenozoic_arabiaeurasia_convergence.pdf
- Total convergence since ~22 Ma (stable northward drift) predicted 440 km; velocity quoted ~19-26 mm/yr overall Arabia-Eurasia, current shortening in Zagros 7-10 mm/yr. Same URL.
- Crust 45 km thick under Zagros Folded Belt and High Zagros (unthickened Arabian margin); Moho 70 km under Sanandaj-Sirjan belt; Iranian block north of SSZ crust ~50 km, warm mantle lithosphere to ~100 km. Same URL.
- Mean Iranian plateau elevation ~1500 m (Hatzfeld & Molnar 2010, as cited); Central Iran crust ~42 km (48 km near Alborz); Zagros/plateau southern edge marked by cumulative topographic step ~2 km. Same URL.
- Basement thrust units spaced ~80 km apart in Fars; topography interpreted as crustal-scale critically tapering wedge; High Zagros is elevated low-relief area morphologically indistinguishable from the plateau edge. Same URL.
- Shortening budget in Fars section: 135 km total = 15 km (5%) in Zagros Folded Belt (post 12.4 Ma), ~50 km duplexing, ~70 km underthrusting below suture (post 25 Ma). 12 km thick sedimentary cover detachment-folded in ZFB (concentric long-wavelength folds). Iranian plateau 29% (38 km) shortening in Central Iran since 10 Ma; plateau 300-450 km wide giving 120-180 km shortening to build current crust. Same URL.
- Te of Arabian plate 50 km (Snyder & Barazangi 1986, as cited); topographic load wavelength ~100 km too small for local Airy. Same URL.
- Exhumation: local High Zagros rates 0.3-0.4 km/Myr; minimum 0.2 km/Myr; sediment accumulation rates in foreland 0.2-0.6 km/Myr. Same URL.
