## 2. Findings per basin

Source limits: Wikipedia and the tubitak/mdpi/uran primary pages could not be fetched (DNS). All values below are [Ö] search summaries unless marked otherwise.

### 2.1 Mediterranean (whole sea; Western/Eastern split pending)
- Area ~2.5 million km2 (0.7% of world ocean). [Ö] search summary, Mediterranean Sea results.
- Average depth 1,500 m. [Ö] several sources, same search.
- Maximum depth CONFLICT: 5,267 m (Calypso Deep, Ionian Sea; older figure) vs 5,109 m +/-1 m (current English Wikipedia infobox, per search snippet). [Ö]
- Messinian salt: CNRS press release (URL from search: cnrs.fr/en/press/how-70-mediterranean-sea-was-lost-55-million-years-ago) says salt layer "up to 3 km thick". Another summary says "more than 2 km" beneath parts of modern sea. Drilling summary says "more than 4,000 ft" (~1.2 km). [Ö] CONFLICT: 3 km vs 2 km vs ~1.2 km.
- Messinian salt volume: Wikipedia Messinian salinity crisis snippet: ~4x10^18 kg, >1 million km3, "may be reduced substantially". [Ö] (Wikipedia snippet via search only).

### 2.2 Western Mediterranean and Alboran (back-arc)
- Alboran Sea: back-arc basin from slab rollback. [Ö] Leuchters dissertation (macau.uni-kiel.de) cited via search: thinned continental crust ~6-7 km in eastern Alboran, grading to oceanic-type crust toward Algerian Basin. [Ö]
- Timing CONFLICT: Oligocene opening of Valencia Trough, Liguro-Provencal and Algero-Balearic basins, then early Tortonian for Alboran and Tyrrhenian (EGU2016-15427) [Ö]; vs early-to-middle Miocene subduction rollback (zendy.io, doi 10.1029/2007GC001639) [Ö]; vs middle-to-late Miocene back-arc oceanic crust in eastern Alboran (same zendy.io) [Ö].
- Crustal thickness: onshore >35 km thinning to ~20 km, then ~16-6 km offshore toward eastern Alboran back-arc (egusphere-2022-1166 preprint). [Ö] preprint, not peer-reviewed in this check.
- Tyrrhenian Sea: opening linked to retreating Calabrian slab ~6 to 2 Ma (core.ac.uk/works/47011256). [Ö]. Crust: serpentinized mantle features (same source) [Ö].

Note on the batch of "area/depth" searches: the query strings contained my own candidate numbers. Only values that the result text itself reports are recorded below; numbers that merely echoed the query were not counted as confirmed.

### 2.3 Eastern Mediterranean (Levant Basin)
- Messinian evaporite thickness: ~2 km basinal maximum (Netzeband seismic work, EGU/Hamburg thesis) [Ö]; up to 3 km (Hsü 1973, as cited) [Ö]; CIESM monograph: mobile unit up to 2 km [Ö]. CONFLICT: 2 vs 3 km. No source found giving ~5000 m for evaporites.
- Water depth at deposition: 2-3 km (CIESM monograph) [Ö].
- Sediment overburden below evaporites: 5-7 km Jurassic-Miocene (EGU05-J-05116); total basin fill 14-16 km (another source) [Ö]. CONFLICT/definition: sediment, not salt.
- Crust CONFLICT: continental (EGU05 abstract for southern basin; CIESM: stretched continental crust) [Ö] vs oceanic (lineated magnetic anomalies, Nature Geoscience 2016, doi ngeo2784) [Ö], formed on Tethys spreading or during Pangaea amalgamation.
- Basin age: NOT FOUND as a single number. Relict Tethys interpretation is the Nature Geoscience line [Ö].

### 2.3b Western Mediterranean Messinian salt (Balearic/Algerian)
- Deep MSC basins: "up to a kilometre" thick in abyssal areas (Dal Cin et al. 2016 context) [Ö] vs "> 1 km" (another) [Ö] vs "1e2 km" (ambiguous text) [Ö]. Not reconciled.
- Balearic Promontory: halite up to 325 m at intermediate depths [Ö].
- Algerian Basin: salt mostly in deep domain floored by oceanic crust [Ö]; thickness cut off in snippet.
- Whole Mediterranean salt volume: ~1.2 x 10^6 km3 (review-based source) [Ö] vs Wikipedia snippet >1 million km3 / 4x10^18 kg [Ö]. Consistent in order of magnitude.

### 2.4 Black Sea
- Area 436,400 km2; mean depth 1,253 m; max depth 2,212 m; volume 547,000 km3 (Wikipedia mirrors via search) [Ö]. Alternative: Britannica 422,000 km2 (sea proper), ~461,000 km2 incl. Sea of Azov [Ö]. Older school pages: max 2,210 m; Romanian morphometry 423,000 km2 [Ö]. Deepest point: Euxine abyssal plain south of Yalta; one older source 2,206 m [Ö].
- Age: Western basin 135-95 Ma; eastern basin ~110 Ma (geophysical study) [Ö].
- Age CONFLICT: back-arc opening 70-60 Ma north of Pontide arc (2002 study, seismic and magnetic) [Ö]; whole basin ~80 Ma (same 2002 study, cited) [Ö].
- Crust CONFLICT: western basin oceanic crust (2002 heat-flow study) [Ö] vs thinned continental crust, possibly Precambrian lithosphere (EGU22-2493) [Ö].
- Eastern basin crust: thinned continental (2002 heat-flow) [Ö]; ~7 km thick, similar to oceanic thickness but lower-than-oceanic velocities (2005 seismic experiment) [Ö]. Scott 2009 PhD (eprints.soton.ac.uk/66348): western basin thinned continental crust ~7 km; eastern basin thick oceanic crust ~13 km. [Ö] (search summary of thesis; thesis itself not fetched).
- Lithosphere 60-65 km for both basins (2002 study) [Ö].

### 2.5 Caspian Sea (South Caspian Basin, SCB)
- Area: 371,000 km2 (Live Science) [Ö]; Britannica 386,400 km2; Kazakh government 376,000 km2 [Ö]. CONFLICT.
- Mean depth: 211 m (Wikipedia infobox per search; A-Z Animals) [Ö]; other sources 184 m, 187 m, 200.5 m [Ö]. CONFLICT.
- Max depth: 1,025 m (most consistent) [Ö]; New World Encyclopedia gives ~980 m in one passage [Ö].
- Origin: Mesozoic extensional setting; island-arc extension early, then Jurassic development, later rifting (possible Eocene rifting) (mdpi Geosciences 15/8/281 via search, 2024) [Ö].
- Crust CONFLICT: <10 km crust beneath ~20 km sediment (2024 study, "cold" SCB) [Ö] vs up to 28.5 km sediment and mostly oceanic crust [Ö] vs oceanic or thinned continental (BP-coauthored abstract) [Ö].
- Timing of opening disputed; one vs two sub-basins disputed [Ö].

### 2.6 Baltic Sea
- Mean depth ~54-55 m; max 459 m (Landsort Deep, Western Gotland Basin), Danish Straits included [Ö].
- Gulf of Bothnia: mean ~60 m, max 295 m; Bothnian Bay mean 43 m, max 147 m [Ö].
- Origin: sources conflict. Gulf of Bothnia described as ancient tectonic depression partly filled with Precambrian and Paleozoic rock [Ö]; another says Baltic basin formed by glacial erosion over recent ice ages [Ö]. No clear date found. Area and sediment thickness: NOT YET FOUND.

### 2.7 Hudson Bay (intracratonic, not a marginal sea; included for completeness)

- Sediment: ~2 km (approx. circular basin) [Ö]; known maximum ~2,500 m Upper Ordovician-Devonian [Ö].
- Origin: intracratonic sag within Canadian Shield; mechanism contested (mantle downwelling vs minor lithospheric thinning in Ordovician-Silurian and Early-Middle Devonian; eclogitization) [Ö].
- Area: NOT YET FOUND.

- Holocene sediment up to 36 m (southern/central Baltic), mostly <6 m north (2025 model, ESD 16, 585) [Ö].

### 2.8 Red Sea
- Oceanic crust: spreading began ~13 Ma along full length (recent analysis) vs ~5 Ma (earlier work), southern/central; northern Red Sea age/extent disputed. [Ö] (Ligi et al. 2011 Geology, KAUST 2021 news summaries).
- Length ~2,250 km rift (Ligi), or 1,900 km long, max width 355 km [Ö]. CONFLICT on dimensions is minor (length definition differs).
- Spreading ultra-slow, 8.3-14.5 mm/yr [Ö].
- Depth: south of ~21N, axial trough 1,000-2,900 m, shelves <50 m; north of 25N, common depth 1,200 m [Ö].
- Area 438,000 km2; mean depth 490 m (Wikipedia infobox per search) [Ö]. Country-table total 491,465 km2 [Ö] (different measure).
- Max depth CONFLICT: 2,730 m (current Wikipedia) [Ö]; 2,850 m (tourism site) [Ö]; 3,040 m (WorldAtlas and older Wikipedia mirror) [Ö]; 2,211 m (older Wikipedia archive) [Ö]; 2,130 m (Spanish defense library) [Ö].
- Shelf width: NOT FOUND.

### 2.9 Sea of Japan (East Sea)
- Back-arc opening: Oligocene to Miocene; early Oligocene to middle Miocene (Tamaki et al., 1992) [Ö]; early Miocene per another summary [Ö]; Korean study first stage ~23-16 Ma, rotation of SW Japan ended 15 Ma [Ö].
- Crust: Yamato Basin ~13 km crust; adjacent shelf off western Noto ~24 km; Yamato Bank ~21 km (continental-character) [Ö].
- Mechanism debated; one hypothesis links to India-Eurasia collision [Ö].
- Area: 1,007,800 km2 (NOAA via Infoplease; Britannica Kids) [Ö]; ~978,000 km2 (Britannica main article and others) [Ö]. CONFLICT.
- Max depth: 3,742 m (Britannica; several sources) [Ö].
- Mean depth CONFLICT: ~1,350 m (NOAA, Britannica Kids) [Ö] vs ~1,752 m (Britannica main, WorldAtlas-derived) [Ö].

### 2.10 South China Sea
- Seafloor spreading start ~33 Ma in NE basin (deep-tow magnetic + IODP Exp. 349) [Ö]; overall ~32-15 Ma [Ö]; end ~20.5 Ma per magnetic modelling (one range) [Ö] (end-age CONFLICT: 20.5 vs 15 Ma).
- Spreading rates 20-80 km/Myr in East Subbasin, ridge jump ~23.6 Ma [Ö].
- Dimensions: V-shaped oceanic basin ~1,200 km long, max width ~800 km [Ö].
- Area ~3,500,000 km2 (Wikipedia infobox per search) [Ö].
- Mean depth 1,212 m (Britannica) [Ö].
- Max depth CONFLICT: 5,016 m (Britannica, tied to China Sea Basin; a geography site places it at Manila Trench) [Ö]; 5,563 m +/-4 m (Wikipedia) [Ö]; 5,559 or 5,567 m (Wikiwand copy) [Ö]; 5,377 m (China Daily) [Ö].

### 2.11 Gulf of Mexico
- Mean depth ~1,615 m; max 3,750-4,384 m (Wikiwand/encyclopedia-style, [Ö]); deepest is Sigsbee Deep [Ö]. Max CONFLICT: 3,750 vs 4,384 m.
- Louann salt (Jurassic): average ~4 km, max ~8 km (gravity inversion study, AAPG abstract 2017 [Ö]); Campeche basin average ~2 km [Ö].
- Oceanic crust: central part averages ~6 km (3-12 km range) [Ö]; spreading Late Jurassic [Ö].
- Spreading end: ~145-150 Ma [Ö]; alternative: late Berriasian (1994 reconstruction, SEPM abstract) [Ö]. Start poorly constrained [Ö].
- Salt deposited over transitional crust before spreading; some salt possibly in partly opened basin, Callovian-early Oxfordian [Ö].
- Area ~1.5-1.6 million km2 (estimates cluster; coastline vs shelf-edge definition differs) [Ö].
- Shelf width: ~280 km off Florida and Yucatan; <10 km at Mississippi Delta (physiographic description, Springer chapter 10.1007/978-1-4939-3447-8_3) [Ö]. Older 1911 Britannica entry gives much narrower values (~130 m Texas-Louisiana; ~120 m W Florida) [Ö], conflicting; treat with caution.
- Shelf share of area: 22% (one source) vs 32% (another); CONFLICT.
- Texas-specific shelf width: BULUNAMADI.
